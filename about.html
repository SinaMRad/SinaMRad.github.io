PROFILE

Pavement & Geotechnical Engineering

I hold a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from Oklahoma State University. My experience includes full-depth and mill-and-inlay pavement design, Pavement Evaluation Summaries (PES) for Georgia Department of Transportation (GDOT) projects, and deep foundation analyses for piles and drilled shafts, including Bridge Foundation Investigations (BFI).

My research background includes asphalt mixture and binder performance testing, Balanced Mix Design (BMD), pavement condition evaluation, and data analysis.

TECHNICAL EXPERTISE

  • Pavement design and condition evaluation: GDOT pavement design tool, PCI, and PAVER
  • Geotechnical analysis: LPILE, APILE, GRLWEAP, and GeoStudio
  • Asphalt materials: Superpave, mixture performance testing, binder rheology, BMD, and recycled asphalt materials
  • Data and design tools: Python, machine learning, JMP, Excel, R, ArcGIS, and AutoCAD
  • Field data collection and technical reporting
