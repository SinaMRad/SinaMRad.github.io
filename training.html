Micro PAVER for Road Pavement Management Systems (PMS)

This course introduces pavement management concepts and the practical use of Micro PAVER for organizing, evaluating, and managing pavement networks. It focuses on translating pavement-condition information into a structured pavement management system that can support maintenance and rehabilitation planning.

  • Fundamentals of pavement management systems and pavement-network organization
  • Pavement condition evaluation and Pavement Condition Index (PCI)
  • Building and managing pavement inventory and inspection data in Micro PAVER
  • Condition analysis, performance evaluation, and maintenance planning
  • Using pavement-management information to support engineering decisions

Road Network Modeling with AIMSUN

This course provides a practical introduction to modeling and simulating road and traffic networks with AIMSUN. It covers the process of building a network model, entering traffic-demand information, defining operational conditions, running simulations, and interpreting results for transportation and traffic-engineering studies.

  • Introduction to traffic-network modeling and the AIMSUN environment
  • Road-network geometry and traffic-demand definition
  • Traffic volumes, origin–destination demand, and network inputs
  • Intersection control, signal timing, and operational scenarios
  • Microscopic simulation and interpretation of traffic-performance outputs
