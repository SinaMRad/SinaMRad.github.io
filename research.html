RESEARCH

Research Areas

My research focuses on asphalt pavement performance and the use of testing, statistics, and machine learning to improve engineering decisions.

01

Balanced Mix Design

Performance-based evaluation of asphalt mixtures using cracking, rutting, and moisture resistance indicators.

02

Cracking & Rutting

Asphalt mixture performance testing including IDEAL-CT and Hamburg Wheel Tracking Test.

03

Moisture Susceptibility

Evaluation of moisture resistance and conditioning effects using laboratory performance tests.

04

Pavement Management

Pavement condition evaluation, PCI, PAVER, field data collection, and maintenance decision support.

05

Asphalt Materials

Superpave, mixture performance testing, binder rheology, recycled asphalt materials, and materials characterization.

06

Data & Machine Learning

Python-based modeling and statistical analysis for pavement materials and cracking performance prediction.
